VRR Departure Dashboard

Saved stops, times and line/direction filters · live departures

Entries store a stop, a daily start time and the exact line/direction combinations you choose. Departures are filtered from that time onward (or from now if the time has already passed today). Data comes from the public VRR EFA service; availability and platform details depend on what the operator supplies.
